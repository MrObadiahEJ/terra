# Terra Book of Intents — Entry Point

This repository is governed by the **Book of Intents**: Terra's living
constitutional record of vision, intent, architecture, implementation status,
decisions, risks, and roadmap. It exists so that any human or AI contributor
can understand Terra and continue development responsibly without repeating
conversations with the founder.

**The Book lives at [`docs/book-of-intents/`](docs/book-of-intents/BOOK_OF_INTENTS.md).**

Start here:

| Order | Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [`docs/book-of-intents/TERRA_VISION.md`](docs/book-of-intents/TERRA_VISION.md) | Authoritative vision and non-negotiable trust principles. |
| 2 | [`docs/book-of-intents/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`](docs/book-of-intents/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) | What is actually built and proven, with evidence. |
| 3 | [`docs/book-of-intents/ROADMAP.md`](docs/book-of-intents/ROADMAP.md) | What comes next and why. |
| 4 | [`docs/book-of-intents/AGENT_GUIDE.md`](docs/book-of-intents/AGENT_GUIDE.md) | How agents and contributors must use and maintain the Book. |

**Core rule:** `OBSERVATION ≠ EVIDENCE ≠ VERIFICATION ≠ CONSENSUS ≠ LEGAL RECOGNITION.`
Terra verifies evidence; it never asserts legal title.
